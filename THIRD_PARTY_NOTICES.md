## Dependencies

Exact Dart versions are in `client/pubspec.lock`; Go requirements and integrity checks are in `server/go.mod` and `server/go.sum`. Native package locks are committed for iOS and macOS. Obtain the applicable license text from the resolved package/module, not from an unversioned search result.

Most inspected dependencies use MIT, BSD or Apache licenses. Linux dependencies `dbus` and `gtk` include MPL-2.0 notices, and some transitive packages include multiple licenses. They must not be relabeled as project-authored code. Preserve their notices and satisfy source-availability requirements that apply to the binaries and modifications you distribute.

Flutter assembles dependency notices during a build. When distributing binaries, include the notices for the exact Flutter/Dart/native dependencies, the Inter license, and the licenses of modules linked into the Go server, including the server payloads embedded in the client. A source checkout and this summary alone are not a complete binary license bundle.

This file records provenance and release requirements; it is not an exhaustive legal review of every transitive dependency or platform SDK.
